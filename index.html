Priority SessionPipe Defect Showdown: Can You Spot the Problem?William Sheppard and Hunter Askew · 1st Floor

This presentation will use real-world sanitary sewer CCTV inspection footage to highlight common pipe defects that can affect the performance and reliability of a sewer collection system. The session is designed to gamify the review process, encouraging attendees to identify and discuss defects as they appear on screen. Through an interactive format, participants will see examples of cracks, fractures, root intrusion, joint defects, deposits, infiltration, and structural deterioration while learning how these conditions can restrict flow, increase maintenance needs, contribute to backups, and lead to system failure.
